🌍 World Trends Dashboard

The world is changing faster than ever — and not always for the better. Rising temperatures, biodiversity loss, conflicts, and shifting economic power reveal a planet under increasing pressure. This dashboard highlights global trends that raise concern.

While national statistics offer valuable detail, only a global perspective shows how all these forces connect — from climate to conflict, from energy to environment. Understanding these linkages is essential to see where the world is headed.

Inspired by the book Earth for All by the Club of Rome, RealityCheck is based on the insight that infinite growth on a finite planet is impossible. The real question is not if, but how quickly we can rethink our path together.

Each chart below visualizes a core global indicator using data from RealityCheck’s integrated database. They serve as reality checks on our collective trajectory — and reminders of what still can change.
