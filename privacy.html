🔒 Privacy Policy – RealityCheck

Protecting your privacy is very important to us. This website does not use cookies and does not collect any personal data.

We do not use analytics tools, advertising networks or third-party tracking. The only information stored locally in your browser is your personal KPI weighting, using localStorage. This is never transmitted to our servers.

The backend stores anonymous server logs (via tracking.php), including:
– Anonymized IP address
– Date and time of access
– Requested page or file
– Browser type and version

These logs are not linked to individuals and are used solely for performance monitoring and abuse prevention.

Some content (such as rankings or summaries) may be generated using artificial intelligence (OpenAI GPT models). However, no personal data is used or shared in this process.

🧩 External Libraries & Services

To display charts, maps and global data, this website uses a few open-source libraries and public data providers:

  • Chart.js – for interactive data visualizations (chartjs.org)
  • Leaflet – for interactive world maps (leafletjs.com)
  • Marked.js – for converting Markdown files in the analysis section (marked.js.org)
  • Our World in Data (OWID) – embedded charts and global datasets (ourworldindata.org)

These resources are loaded directly from their respective content delivery networks (CDNs) or providers. When a library or embedded chart is loaded, your browser may technically transmit your IP address to that provider to deliver the file. RealityCheck itself does not perform any tracking or store personal information from these services.

You have the right to request access to any data stored about you and request its deletion at any time. Contact: ckvfox@gmail.com
