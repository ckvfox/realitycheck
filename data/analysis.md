## Executive Verdict

The measured world has become **less safe** over the past five years, driven by increased geopolitical risks, conflict incidences, and state-based conflict deaths. From 2020 to 2025, armed conflicts increased by 22.2%, and state-based conflict deaths more than doubled. However, some areas like environmental resilience and socio-economic metrics show mixed trends, suggesting room for optimism. Confidence in these conclusions is high given the substantial data coverage.

## Security and Instability

- **Armed Conflicts and Deaths**: The number of armed conflicts rose to **55** (+22.2%), with conflict deaths reaching **153,643** (+110.1%) in 2025, indicating a significant spike in instability.
- **Geopolitical Risks**: The Geopolitical Risk Index surged to **173.6** (+91.57), signaling heightened tensions.
- **Military Spending**: Increased globally by **25.4%** to **$2771.29bn**, reflecting rising security concerns.

## Climate Change and Measured Impacts

- **Physical Changes**: The global temperature anomaly increased to **1.39°C** above pre-industrial levels by 2026 (+0.26°C). **Global CO₂ emissions** were up by **4.1%**, highlighting ongoing environmental challenges.
- **Climate-related Disasters**: Recorded damage remained steady, with notable rises in deaths, yet the people affected decreased by **-15.5%**, signaling variable impact severity.
- **Adaptation and Resilience**: The **number of natural disasters** dropped by **-12.1%**, and there was a **slight improvement** in the Living Planet Index, suggesting progress in ecosystem management.

## Country and Group Patterns

- **Country Extremes**: **Nigeria** and **India** saw notable population increases, while **Ukraine** faced a dramatic decline. **Russia** and **Ukraine** held the highest peace index scores, underlining regional tension (Global Peace Index 2025).
- **Group Trends**: The EU, G7, and ASEAN maintain relatively stable socio-economic indicators, but the African Union shows weaker governance and economic outcomes (e.g., health expenditure per capita).

## Noteworthy Changes and Outliers

- **Anomalies**: The **Dominican Republic**'s land area surged over **200%**, worth verifying. **Lebanon** saw the sharpest decline in inflation rates (-70.27 percentage points).
- **Record Inflows**: **Germany** experienced a massive increase in refugees hosted (+119.3%).
- **Declines**: **Japan**'s GDP per capita fell markedly, reflecting economic challenges in contrast to its typically strong economic stability.

## Potential Best Practices

- **High Immunization**: Countries like **Turkey** and **Samoa** managed substantial improvements in immunization coverage.
- **Renewables**: The **Democratic Republic of the Congo** achieves an exceptional renewable energy share, potentially offering lessons in sustainable energy systems.

## Connections and Hypotheses

- **Economy ↔ CO₂**: A Spearman correlation of **+1.00** between GDP and CO₂ emissions suggests potential links between economic growth and environmental costs.
- **Democracy ↔ Happiness**: **Democracy Index** correlates positively with the **World Happiness Index** (rho=+0.87), hinting at governance's role in societal contentment.
- **Poverty and Sanitation**: Access to basic sanitation correlates negatively with **poverty levels** (rho=-0.85), highlighting foundational infrastructure’s role in livelihood upliftment.

## Economy

- **Real GDP per Capita Growth**: Despite nominal GDP increasing generally, real GDP growth remains moderate with group differences; ASEAN leads growth at **3.65%**. Inflation emerged as a significant challenge, rising to a median of **3.06%** (+1.13 percentage points).

## Society and Governance

- **Governance and Trust**: Global metrics show mild improvement in government effectiveness, yet democracy and human rights indices declined slightly, warranting vigilance.
- **Women's Representation**: Increased by **3.89 percentage points** to **26.47%**, showing slow but positive progress in gender equality.

## Technology

- **Internet and Mobile Connectivity**: Substantial improvement in internet penetration (**+15.26%**) and mobile use, expanding global connectivity but uneven across regions.
- **Electric Vehicles**: Rapid expansion to **76 million vehicles** underscores a transformative shift in transport sectors.

## Data Limits and Outlook

- **Coverage and Recency**: Thin coverage in some newer KPIs limits establishing definitive trends. Data points for critical issues like the **Global Peace Index** are comprehensive, enhancing confidence.
- **Outlook**: While economic and technological advancements offer growth potential, rising geopolitical tensions and environmental sustainability challenges require innovative policy and cooperation to ensure a safe, prosperous future worldwide. The focus should be on flipping robust economic methods into sustainable environmental adaptations.
