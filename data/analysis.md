## Executive verdict
The measured world has become less safe over the past five years, from 2020 to 2025. This assessment is characterized by rising geopolitical risks, increasing displacement, and a surge in state-based conflict deaths. Confidence in this verdict remains moderate due to mixed progression in socio-economic and environmental KPIs, suggesting that instability is not solely driven by conflict.

## Security and instability
- **State-based conflict deaths** have surged by **110.1%** since 2020 (Global KPI).
- **Geopolitical risks** have escalated markedly, with the index rising from **82.07 to 152.7** (Global KPI).
- There is a notable increase in **forced displacements**, now at **119.25m people**, a **46.3%** rise since 2020 (Global KPI).
- The **number of armed conflicts** worldwide has risen from **45 to 55** (Global KPI), highlighting broad-based instability beyond localized troubles. 

## Climate change and measured impacts
- **Global CO₂ emissions** have increased by **4.1%** since 2019, reaching **38.60bn tonnes** in 2024 (Global KPI).
- **Global temperature anomaly** has increased by **0.27°C** since 2021 (Global KPI), manifesting visible signs of climate shifts.
- Despite a **-12.1%** decrease in **recorded natural disasters** since 2020, the five-year average for **climate-related disaster deaths** has increased by **427.4%** (Global KPI), warranting attention to adaptive measures.

## Country and group patterns
- **BRICS** nations see varied fortunes, with a mixed economic outlook and moderate environmental performance (**Environmental Performance Index median 36.35** in 2022).
- The **African Union** remains challenged in socio-economic development, evident in low GDP per capita and high poverty levels.
- **OECD** countries show high scores in both economic prosperity and social well-being, with notable infrastructure performance and lower inequality.

## Noteworthy changes and outliers
- **Ukraine** exhibited a substantial increase in **military spending**, up by **30.41 percentage points** since 2019, indicating intensified efforts in national defense.
- **Germany** has drastically increased its number of **refugees hosted by 119.3%** from 2020 to 2025.
- **Bangladesh** recorded a significant decline in **air quality PM2.5 exposure** by **12.7%** from 2018 (KPI: air_quality_pm2_5_exposure).

## Potential best practices
- **Finland** and **Denmark** showcase robust performances in environmental indexes and social progress, possibly deriving from sustained governance models and green policies.
- **Rwanda's** significant strides in electrification, with an increase of **25.40 percentage points** since 2019, can serve as a case study for rapid infrastructure development.

## Connections and hypotheses
- **Economic growth (nominal GDP)** is commonly associated with increased **CO₂ emissions**, indicating further industrialization and energy consumption.
- A positive association exists between **democracy indicators** and **happiness levels**, pointing to governance quality as a potential influence on societal well-being.

## Economy and labor
- World GDP saw nominal growth; however, real prosperity assessments remain hampered by inflation considerations (KPI: gdp_per_capita_current_us).
- Improvements are observed in **employment-to-population ratios** and **female labor force participation**, reflecting efforts toward enhanced labor market inclusivity.

## Society and governance
- There is a slight global decline in the **Democracy Index**, with deteriorating trust and media freedoms in several countries such as **Afghanistan** and **Nicaragua**.
- **Women in Parliament** representation has increased globally by **3.89 percentage points**, indicating progressive gender equity in leadership roles.

## Technology
- Rapid advancements in **electric vehicle stocks** mark a notable shift toward greener technologies, rising **645.1%** since 2020 (Global KPI).
- The cross-country **internet penetration rate** has increased substantially by **15.26 percentage points** since 2019, suggesting digital growth.
